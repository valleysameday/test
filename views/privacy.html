Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. RCT‑X is built for the Rhondda, and we treat your data with the same respect we’d expect ourselves.

Why We Process Your Data (Lawful Basis)

Under the UK GDPR, we process your information to fulfill our contract with you (providing the marketplace service) and for our legitimate interests in keeping the platform secure, preventing fraud, and supporting the local community.

What We Collect

We only collect the information needed to run the platform safely and smoothly:

  • Account Info: Your name and email address when you register.
  • Content: Your listings, messages sent through the platform, and saved items.
  • Technical Data: Basic usage data (like your IP address) to prevent abuse and keep the platform secure.

What We Don’t Do

  • No Selling: We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • No Advertisers: We do not share your information with external advertising networks.
  • No Tracking: We don’t use invasive tracking cookies or follow you across the web.

How Long We Keep It

We only keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you choose to close your account, your personal data will be deleted or anonymised within 60 days, unless we are required by UK law to retain it for longer (for example, for tax records or legal compliance).

Your Rights & Complaints

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time.

  • To make a data request, please contact us at support@rctx.co.uk.
  • In accordance with the Data Use and Access Act 2025, we will acknowledge all data-related complaints within 30 days.
  • If you feel we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
