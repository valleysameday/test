About RCT‑X

RCT‑X is built for one place and one place only — the Rhondda.

No big‑city algorithms. No hidden fees. No confusing menus. Just a simple, local marketplace where people can buy, sell, swap, and support each other.

Every feature, every button, every bit of this platform is designed with the valleys in mind. Whether you're clearing out the house, hunting for a bargain, or supporting a small local seller, RCT‑X is here to make it easy.

Built in the Rhondda. Built for the Rhondda.
